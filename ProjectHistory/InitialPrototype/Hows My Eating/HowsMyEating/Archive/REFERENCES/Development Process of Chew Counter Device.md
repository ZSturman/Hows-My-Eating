### Objective

Design and develop a wearable device that monitors and analyzes eating habits using sound recognition technology.

### Components Required

- **Microphone:** To capture sounds related to eating.
- **Bluetooth Transmitter:** For wireless data communication.
- **Battery:** To power the device independently.
- **On/Off Switch:** For user control of device operation.
- **Charging Port:** To recharge the device's battery.

### Development Steps

1. **Prototype Design:**
    - Craft a clay mold for ear-fit testing to ensure comfort and functionality.
2. **Microphone Integration:**
    - Develop a microphone capable of distinctly capturing sounds of different eating actions (chewing, swallowing, talking).
3. **Data Collection:**
    - Record various eating sounds (hard food, soft food, liquids) and verbal interactions.
4. **Data Analysis:**
    - Convert audio recordings into visual data representations to identify unique patterns and possible classifications.
5. **Notification System:**
    - Design notifications for different detected activities; test for accuracy and reliability.
6. **Parameter Setting:**
    - Define algorithms to detect key eating actions such as the start of a meal, count of chews, and instances of swallowing.
7. **Bluetooth Integration:**
    - Establish a method for sending collected data to a connected device via Bluetooth for further processing or user feedback.
