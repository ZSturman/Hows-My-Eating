### Objective

Program the Chew Tracker device to accurately monitor and analyze different eating patterns based on the type of food consumed.

### Time Between Bites

- **Standard Time Interval:** 30 seconds between bites to ensure consistent data capture.

### Chew Count by Food Type

1. **Soft Foods:** 5-10 chews (e.g., bananas, yogurt, mashed potatoes)
2. **Cooked Vegetables:** 10-15 chews (e.g., steamed carrots, broccoli)
3. **Tender Meats:** 15-20 chews (e.g., chicken, fish)
4. **Tougher Meats:** 20-30 chews (e.g., steak, pork)
5. **Dense Foods:** 30-40 chews (e.g., nuts, raw vegetables)

### Bite Size Specifications

1. **Soft/Cooked Vegetables and Fruits:** Bite-size pieces, approximately 1/2 inch (1.3 cm) to 1 inch (2.5 cm)
2. **Meats, Poultry, and Fish:** Cube or thin slice, 1/2 inch (1.3 cm) to 1 inch (2.5 cm)
3. **Breads and Baked Goods:** Small bites, around 1 inch (2.5 cm) or smaller
4. **Pasta, Rice, and Other Grains:** Small forkfuls or spoonfuls, comfortably fitting in the mouth
